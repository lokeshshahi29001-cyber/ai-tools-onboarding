### Summary

Kieran Flanagan explains how AI is reshaping marketing teams by reducing specialized roles and creating broader, AI-enabled skill sets. He identifies four key marketing archetypes for the future: the Narrator, who differentiates brands through storytelling and positioning; the Tastemaker, who uses creative judgment to stand out; the Grower, who combines marketing with AI-powered technical skills to drive growth; and the Connector, who builds meaningful human relationships through communities and events. ### Key Takeaways

- AI will consolidate traditional marketing roles into broader, multidisciplinary skill sets.  
- Storytelling, positioning, and creativity remain key competitive advantages that AI cannot replace.  
- Modern growth marketers should combine marketing expertise with AI and technical skills.  
- Human connection through communities and events will become increasingly valuable in an AI-driven world.  
- Future marketing teams should balance AI efficiency with uniquely human judgment and relationship-building.

### Summary

Kieran Flanagan argues that the most effective marketing leaders in the AI era must excel at two core capabilities: building owned audiences and scaling AI-powered workflows. As AI increases the volume of content and competition for attention, marketers should prioritize channels they control—such as newsletters, podcasts, communities, and events—to build lasting relationships. - Human creativity and strategic judgment become more important as AI-generated content becomes widespread.  
- Successful B2B marketing teams will balance owned media strategies with AI-powered operational efficiency.
