**Date:** 2 weeks ago

### Summary

Amanda Natividad explains that an effective marketing strategy should always begin by understanding how a business is designed to grow. She outlines three common growth models—discovery-led, product-led, and sales-led—and explains how each requires a different marketing approach. Rather than trying to execute every marketing tactic at once, she recommends identifying the quickest opportunity to demonstrate marketing's value and build trust within the organization before expanding into long-term strategic initiatives.

- Build internal trust first, then expand into broader long-term marketing programs.

## YouTube Resource

**Video Title:** Why B2B Is MORE Emotional Than B2C

**Video URL: [https://youtu.be/gK-f80dTjxM?si=L4zINz_rKf_eOfae](https://youtu.be/gK-f80dTjxM?si=L4zINz_rKf_eOfae)**

### Summary

Amanda Natividad interviews conversion optimization expert Talia Wolf about emotional targeting in B2B marketing. They explain that B2B buying decisions are often more emotional than B2C purchases because buyers face career risk, budget responsibility, and internal pressure. The discussion covers customer research, qualitative analysis, comparison pages, emotional messaging, and how understanding customer motivations leads to higher conversions. They also explore how Zero Click Marketing and emotional targeting complement each other by focusing on delivering genuine value before asking customers to buy.

### Key Takeaways

- B2B purchasing decisions are heavily influenced by emotions such as trust, risk, and credibility.  
- Customer research and qualitative insights are essential for creating messaging that truly resonates.  
- Comparison pages should focus on solving customer problems rather than simply listing product features.  
- Businesses should own their narrative by publishing transparent, helpful content instead of letting AI or third parties define it.  
- Emotional targeting and Zero Click Marketing work together to build trust, authority, and long-term customer relationships.
