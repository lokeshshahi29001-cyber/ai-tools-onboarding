### Summary

Kyle Coleman explains that successful AI adoption is driven by culture, not technology. He required every member of his marketing team to share one AI use case each week, regardless of how small the improvement was. By personally reviewing and responding to every submission, he reinforced the importance of experimentation and continuous learning. Within six weeks, AI usage increased 20× across the organization, eventually leading to more than 230 documented AI workflows across 13 teams. Kyle argues that building AI habits and managing organizational change are the real drivers of long-term AI transformation.

### Key Takeaways

- Consistent experimentation builds lasting AI adoption across teams.
- Leadership involvement is essential to creating a culture of AI innovation.
- Successful AI transformation depends on change management, not just technology.

### Summary

Kyle Coleman shares the five-phase framework his marketing organization used to transform AI from an occasional productivity tool into a core operating system. Starting with a simple weekly AI experimentation mandate, the team gradually built an AI-first culture, a centralized workflow library, cross-functional automation systems, and personalized AI coaching plans. Over eight months, the organization created 230 documented AI workflows across 13 teams, dramatically improving marketing efficiency and scalability. - Treat AI implementation as an organizational transformation, not just a technology upgrade.
