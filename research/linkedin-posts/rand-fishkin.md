## About

Rand Fishkin is the Co-founder and CEO of SparkToro and the former Co-founder of Moz. He is one of the most influential voices in SEO, audience research, and digital marketing. His work focuses on helping marketers understand where audiences spend time online, build stronger brands, and make data-driven marketing decisions beyond traditional search metrics.

### Summary

Rand Fishkin explains that influence in today's digital landscape happens across many platforms—not just search engines or company websites. Using Similarweb's analysis of the top 100 websites, he shows that buyers spend most of their time on social media, commerce platforms, news sites, communities, and other third-party channels. He argues that marketers must embrace the "Zero-Click Web" by building visibility where audiences already consume information instead of relying solely on driving website traffic.

### Key Takeaways

- Modern customer influence happens across multiple platforms, not just search engines.  
- Buyers research brands on social media, communities, commerce sites, and other third-party platforms before visiting a company website.  
- Marketing strategies should prioritize building visibility where audiences already spend their time.  
- The Zero-Click Web requires brands to earn attention beyond owned websites and traditional SEO.  
- Brand discovery increasingly happens on platforms companies do not directly control.

## YouTube Resource

**Video Title:** Rand Fishkin: AI Search Rankings Don't Make Sense, Here's What Actually Works

**Video URL: [https://youtu.be/auH1_MeauBM?si=cCVXv75N9Sr96TfY](https://youtu.be/auH1_MeauBM?si=cCVXv75N9Sr96TfY)**

### Summary

Rand Fishkin explains why AI search visibility is fundamentally different from traditional SEO. He argues that AI does not produce fixed rankings but probabilistic recommendations, making "AI rankings" misleading. Instead of chasing rankings, marketers should focus on brand visibility, audience trust, and being consistently mentioned across authoritative websites, communities, and media. The discussion also explores zero-click marketing, AI's limitations, brand building, and how marketing success increasingly depends on influencing perception rather than generating website clicks.

### Key Takeaways

- AI does not have fixed rankings; brand visibility matters more than position.
- Strong brand mentions across trusted websites, communities, Reddit, YouTube, and news sources improve AI recommendations.
- Zero-click marketing is becoming the norm, making brand perception more valuable than website traffic alone.
